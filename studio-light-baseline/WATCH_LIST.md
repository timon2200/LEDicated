# Video watch list for the nine-bar room

Watch in this order. The videos teach the methods; several use **5 V strips, different ESP32 boards or older WLED screens**. Use the project's 24 V WS2814 and ESP32-C3 specifications for the actual build.

1. **[The Ultimate LED Strip Power Injection Guide — QuinLED / Intermit.Tech](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Umo6jKLfwsQ)** (about an hour). Best detailed explanation of power, voltage drop, cable size, fuses and injection calculations. The creator's [companion article and diagrams](https://quinled.info/the-ultimate-led-strip-power-injection-guide/) are useful for doing our measured nine-bar numbers. Its Dig-Quad example is a teaching aid; the [current pre-assembled product page warns about a 24 V board issue](https://quinled.info/quinled-dig-quad/).
2. **[The RIGHT Way to Join LED Strips and Do Power Injection — The Hook Up](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WGgzWWrCIhY).** Shows cutting, soldering and power connections for IP30 addressable strips, relevant when turning three 5 m rolls into nine 1.2 m bars. Also see the creator's [written walkthrough](https://www.thesmarthomehookup.com/the-right-way-to-join-led-strips-and-do-power-injection/).
3. **[DIY LED Strip Music Reactive Doorway — Made With Light](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZDrc9N3FIq0)** (WLED begins about 7:21; LedFx about 11:07). A practical one-controller example of WLED receiving music effects from LedFx. Its LED strip is **5 V SK6812**, so do not copy its power wiring for our 24 V strip.
4. **[How to Record Internal Audio on Mac | BlackHole 2ch — Relyt](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ayupHGpCtx0)** (Multi-Output Device at about 1:40). Shows the Mac audio split we need for **YouTube → speakers + LedFx**. Follow BlackHole's [current official guide](https://github.com/ExistentialAudio/BlackHole/wiki/Getting-Started%3A-Creating-a-Multi-Output-Device) if macOS menus differ.
5. **[WLED Sync Explained — HandyDadTV](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Yd-2Sl8v5FM).** Useful for understanding presets and fallback synchronization across controller nodes. For the main music show, LedFx or a central scene engine will be the single sender.
6. **[LEDs with TouchDesigner — TouchDesigner Tutorial 208](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5HbLlFqNjfg).** Advanced stage: map camera/audio visuals across a physical LED layout. This is a potential creative tool when the nine-bar hardware and LedFx baseline work; it is more programming-heavy than the first build.

**Suggested first evening:** videos 1, 3 and 4. The first physical milestone is one 120 cm bar that responds to MacBook YouTube audio through WLED and LedFx.
